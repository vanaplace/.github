## About Us

VANAPLACE is a South Korea-based company dedicated to discovering and sharing exceptional products, authentic craftsmanship, and meaningful experiences.

We believe that truly good things carry the stories of their origins, the dedication of their makers, and values that stand the test of time.

From the olive groves of Crete to the traditions of Modena, we bring carefully selected products and their stories to everyday life.

**Our philosophy is simple: Bring the finest standards from distant places to your everyday table.**

## What We Do

- **Curated Products** — Carefully selected olive oils, balsamic vinegars, and other exceptional essentials.
- **Authentic Experiences** — Connecting people with the stories, traditions, and craftsmanship behind every product.
- **Digital Innovation** — Building and improving the technology that supports our brand, services, and customer experiences.

## Engineering at VANAPLACE

This GitHub organization is the home of VANAPLACE's software development and technical projects.

We believe in building technology that is simple, reliable, scalable, and thoughtfully designed.
